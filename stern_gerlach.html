Stern-Gerlach analyzer

Silver atoms (spin ½) leave the oven, pass through up to three analyzers, and are counted in the + or − channel. Rotate each analyzer and choose which channel feeds the next one.

Axis angle θ is measured in the x–z plane from +z toward +x, so θ = 0° is +z, 90° is +x, 180° is −z and 270° is −x. In the diagram the + channel is always drawn upward and the rotating magnet shows the analyzer's axis. For an atom polarized along angle φ, the probability of exiting the + channel is cos²[(θ − φ)/2]. An atom leaving the + channel is polarized along θ; one leaving the − channel is polarized opposite (θ + 180°). Any prior polarization is erased by a measurement, so changing any setting resets the counts.
